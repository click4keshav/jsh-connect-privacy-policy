Privacy Policy — JSH Connect

Effective Date: October 15, 2026 | Last Updated: October 15, 2026

Welcome to JSH Connect ("we", "our", or "us"). JSH Connect is a community platform intended to connect residents (निवासी) and non-resident natives (प्रवासी) of Jaswantgarh. The app may provide community updates, civic suggestions, local directory information, cultural history, job opportunities, job applications, and community opinion polls.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, how it may be shared, and the choices available to users when using JSH Connect.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account and Profile Information: When you register or sign in, we may collect your email address, display name, unique username, residency status (Gaon Niwasi or Pravasi), and profile privacy preferences.
  • User-Generated Content: We store content you submit through the app, which may include posts, comments, votes, job listings, job applications, poll responses, and related activity.
  • Images and Media: If you choose to attach an image to a post, the selected image may be uploaded to our image-hosting provider, Cloudinary, to display it within the app.
  • Advertising and Device Information: If Google AdMob is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location where permitted, and diagnostic or interaction data, subject to their policies and your device settings.
  • App Activity and Notifications: The app may process activity information needed to provide features such as follower notifications, votes, and poll reminders. Some notifications or activity records may be stored locally on your device.
  • Technical Information: Our service providers may process technical information necessary for authentication, security, troubleshooting, and reliable operation of the app.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Create, authenticate, and manage user accounts.
  • Display profiles, posts, comments, directory entries, job listings, and other community content.
  • Enable voting, polls, and related integrity checks.
  • Provide notifications and relevant app features.
  • Moderate content and address reports or violations.
  • Protect the app against spam, misuse, and unauthorized access.
  • Diagnose technical problems and improve app reliability.
  • Display advertisements if advertising is enabled.

3. Profile Privacy and Public Content

Depending on the features available in the app, users may be able to select Public or Private profile settings.

When a profile is private, access to follower and following lists may be restricted. This setting does not necessarily make community posts, comments, directory entries, or other content public by default or private by default; their visibility depends on the relevant feature and settings.

Please avoid posting sensitive personal information. Information you publish in public areas of the app may be visible to other users and may be copied or shared by them.

4. Third-Party Services and Data Sharing

JSH Connect may use the following third-party services to operate its features:

  • Supabase: Database hosting and authentication. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Cloudinary: Image hosting and delivery. Cloudinary Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: In-app advertising, if enabled. Google Privacy Policy

These providers may process information as needed to deliver their services and in accordance with their applicable terms and privacy policies.

We do not sell or rent users' personal information to third-party marketers. Information may also be disclosed when necessary to protect users, address security issues, comply with applicable law, or respond to lawful requests.

5. Content Moderation and Safety

Authorized moderators may review, flag, or hide content reported as inappropriate. Administrators may remove content or restrict accounts that violate applicable community rules. Information necessary to investigate reports or enforce these rules may be processed for these purposes.

6. Data Security

We rely on appropriate technical and organizational safeguards and our service providers' security features to protect information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain account information and user content for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, comply with applicable obligations, and resolve disputes.

Where the app provides a delete-post feature, you may use it to remove your posts. To request deletion of your account and associated personal information, contact us using the email address provided below.

Please include your account username and enough information to help us identify the account. Do not send your password. We may need to verify your request before processing it. Some information may be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

8. Children's Privacy

JSH Connect is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate authorization, please contact us so that we can review the request and take appropriate action.

9. Your Choices and Rights

Subject to applicable law and the features available in the app, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. You can also review your profile privacy settings and manage advertising preferences through your device settings where supported.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy as the app's features, service providers, or legal requirements change. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
